## Suggested manual test steps

1. Run `dotnet run` from the repository root.
2. Open the tray menu's **About** item and confirm it shows Version 1.4 and states that Marmalade and Merry share behavior rules.
3. Select **Marmalade**. Observe idle and autonomous movement for several minutes; move the pointer near and moderately far from the pet to exercise mouse-look and curious walking.
4. Use **Call Pet**, **Do Something**, and **Rest**. Single-click, double-click slowly enough to remain separate reactions, click three times as a petting streak, then pick up and drop the pet. Note movement pacing, animation pacing, reaction types, and energy changes in diagnostics.
5. Select **Merry** and repeat step 3 and step 4. Confirm the artwork and active tray checkmark change, while the available reactions and overall behavior rules/pacing match Marmalade.
6. Open `%LOCALAPPDATA%\MarmaladeDesktopPet\diagnostics.log` and confirm the session reports `version=1.4`, both `PET_SWITCH` entries appear, state/energy/click/drag events continue to log, and Merry still triggers atlas comparison diagnostics.
7. Quit from the tray menu and relaunch once with Merry saved as active; confirm it starts normally with Merry artwork and the shared behavior baseline.
