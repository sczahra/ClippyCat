1. `(74, 47)-(386, 462)`
2. `(453, 42)-(819, 463)`
3. `(875, 73)-(1256, 468)`
4. `(1308, 49)-(1668, 468)`
5. `(38, 481)-(387, 883)`

The components are visibly four frames across the upper band and two across the lower band. At full alpha sensitivity, the same six cats are the six overwhelmingly largest 8-connected nontransparent components (80,087-99,965 pixels each); the next-largest separate artifact is only 361 pixels. Some low-alpha fringes overlap the nominal horizontal band split, so extraction will use these six connected-component masks rather than a destructive hard crop. Sorting the components as four upper then two lower frames gives an unambiguous row-major animation order without reconstructing or creatively altering artwork.

## Integration plan

1. Segment the six largest 8-connected nontransparent components and sort them as four upper then two lower frames in row-major order.
2. Isolate each frame from its complete connected visible-art mask and bounds, retaining all connected antialiased edge pixels while excluding unrelated disconnected specks.
3. Scale each frame proportionally (no distortion) to fit within a maximum 182x198 content area, center it horizontally, and align its visible bottom to y=203 in a transparent 192x208 cell. This matches the established atlas margins/baseline while preserving the approved pose and alpha.
4. Append atlas row 11 to Marmalade, producing a 1536x2496 RGBA image. Place the six converted frames in columns 0-5 and leave columns 6-7 fully transparent.
5. Preserve Marmalade rows 0-10 pixel-for-pixel.
6. Expand Merry to 1536x2496 RGBA, preserving rows 0-10 pixel-for-pixel and leaving all of row 11 transparent as a reserved future Stretch row.
7. Add row 11 / six-frame Stretch constants and a real Stretch state only for rendering Marmalade's approved animation.
8. Keep the shared tray/`TriggerAction`/dispatcher path. Marmalade Stretch enters the new state; Merry Stretch shows `Stretch animation coming soon.` Scratch continues to show its coming-soon message for both pets.
9. Add Stretch conservatively to Marmalade-only **Do Something** and autonomous selection. Merry and Scratch will never generate placeholder popups randomly. Preserve all existing action thresholds and take the small Stretch share only from Idle.
10. Update About to Version 1.8, minimally update README, build, and validate dimensions, alpha, row population, cell containment, and unchanged legacy rows.
