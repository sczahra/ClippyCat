## Classification

**Silhouette/artwork difference.**

The frame is already horizontally centered to within 0.5-1.0 px and bottom-aligned to within 1-2 px. Merry's added transparent margin is nearly symmetric, so the mismatch is not caused by a misplaced crop or padding offset. The visual width is substantially affected by Marmalade's broader body, whiskers, and wider tail silhouette, while Merry is naturally slimmer.

The width and height ratios also do not support a clean uniform transform: matching Marmalade's width would make Merry too tall, while matching height would leave a meaningful width difference. A non-uniform scale would distort the character, and changing the tail/body/whisker extent would require artistic judgment or repainting.
## Edit decision

Row 8, frame 0 **was not edited**. No geometric operation was performed. The conservative no-edit result avoids distorting Merry's pose or introducing frame-to-frame scale popping without inspecting other frames, which are explicitly out of scope.

Because the atlas was not modified:

- all pixels inside and outside row 8, frame 0 remain unchanged
- transparency and the 192 x 208 cell remain unchanged
- the atlas remains 1536 x 2288
- Merry's shadow, white coat, gray head patch, and eye colors remain unchanged

## Acceptance and artistic recommendation

The frame is acceptable as character-specific artwork. The measurement difference is real, but it is not a clearly safe alignment-only fix. If exact cross-character silhouette parity is required later, this frame should be handled through deliberate image editing with animation-context review rather than automatic geometry.

Temporary focused extracts and the comparison image are stored in `codex-checkpoints/v1.5-analysis/`.
