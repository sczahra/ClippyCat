No existing frame was relabeled or reused as Scratch.

## Scratch attempt

- Requested exactly eight Marmalade poses in a 4x2 row-major sequence: neutral, leg lift, reach, contact A, contact B, contact A, lower, recovery.
- The first generated image was 1774x887 RGB with a baked gray checkerboard and no alpha channel.
- A single targeted background-extraction edit was requested, changing only the background to genuine transparency.
- The edited result was also 1774x887 RGB with a baked checkerboard and no alpha channel.
- The progression was broadly legible, but the face, head-to-body ratio, and overall roundness drifted toward a more generic/cartoon-like cat than the production artwork.
- Because both alpha and identity/style requirements failed, the grid was not segmented into source frames.
