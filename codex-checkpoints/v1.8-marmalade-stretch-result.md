## Stretch behavior

- `PetState.Stretch` now represents a real dedicated animation state.
- Stretch uses atlas row 11 with six frames, a 220 ms frame delay, and a randomized 1.8-2.6 second state duration.
- Stretch has no horizontal movement and uses the existing one-point light-action energy handling.
- The tray command continues through `TriggerAction(PetAction.Stretch)` and the shared `PerformPetAction` dispatcher.
- With Marmalade active, the dispatcher enters the real Stretch state.
- With Merry active, the dispatcher does not enter the empty row and instead shows `Stretch animation coming soon.`

## Random-action behavior

- **Do Something** includes Stretch only for Marmalade (one of six eligible implemented actions). Merry retains the original five-action pool, so it never receives a random coming-soon popup.
- Autonomous Stretch is Marmalade-only and conservative at 3% for normal and high energy.
- That share is taken only from Idle: Marmalade Idle is 31% at normal energy and 9% at high energy, while Merry retains the original 34% and 12% Idle probabilities.
- All other action thresholds remain unchanged, and tired behavior does not select Stretch.
