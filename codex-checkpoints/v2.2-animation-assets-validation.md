## Visual checks

- Sit/Settle: both pets move from upright sitting into a progressively lower, calmer seated posture; identity, scale, camera, and baseline remain consistent.
- Tail Flick: both pets remain seated and stable while the tail supplies the visible motion. Marmalade generation attempt 1 was rejected because frame 3's tail tip touched the source-panel edge. The targeted second attempt corrected the crop and is the accepted pack.
- Roll/Flop: both pets follow the requested eight-frame lean, shoulder contact, side flop, partial roll, and relaxed recovery. Anatomy is intact and the motion reads as settling rather than tumbling.
- Merry's gray crown patch and green anatomical-left / blue anatomical-right eyes remain consistent in the accepted previews.
- No text, labels, borders, floor, cast shadow, or checkerboard is present in any source frame. Frame numbers and checkerboard appear only in the review previews.

## Transparency recovery

The generated source sheets used a uniform magenta chroma background rather than alpha. A conservative edge-connected saturated-magenta mask removed only the background, after which each silhouette was scaled uniformly per action pack, centered, and placed on a transparent 192x208 RGBA canvas. No checkerboard recovery was required. The accepted frames show no visible loss of fur, whiskers, paws, white markings, or tail tips.

All six action packs pass the pre-integration quality gate.
