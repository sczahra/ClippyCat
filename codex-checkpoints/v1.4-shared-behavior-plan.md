## Merry-specific behavior differences found in `Program.cs`

- Pet switching resets Merry to 84 energy and Marmalade to 72.
- Walking costs Merry 1 energy per tick and Marmalade 2.
- Jumping and pawing cost Merry 2 energy per tick and Marmalade 3.
- Resting restores Merry 6 energy per tick and Marmalade 5.
- Merry checks cursor curiosity every 500 ms and Marmalade every 650 ms.
- Merry's close-cursor attention chance uses an energy threshold of 45 and chances of 78%/52%; Marmalade uses a threshold of 55 and chances of 64%/36%.
- Merry's longer-range curious-walk chance is 26%; Marmalade's is 17%.
- Merry at 60+ energy uses a dedicated active-state distribution; Marmalade uses the common 75+ active distribution and the common mid-energy distribution below that.
- Merry's idle state has a 5,200 ms maximum; Marmalade's maximum is 7,000 ms.
- Merry's normal walking speed is randomly 3-4 pixels per tick; Marmalade's is 2-3.
- Merry's short curious-walk speed is 3 pixels per tick; Marmalade's is 2.
- Merry's called-walk speed is 5 pixels per tick; Marmalade's is 4.
- Merry's walk animation frame delay is 90 ms; Marmalade's is 105 ms.
- Merry's first-click reaction weights are always paw 34%, jump 24%, mouse-look 20%, wave 14%, groom 8%. Marmalade instead uses energy-sensitive reactions: below 30 energy, groom 50%, mouse-look 30%, wave 20%; otherwise wave 36%, paw 24%, mouse-look 19%, jump 14%, groom 7%.

The other Merry references in `Program.cs` select the active pet, update menu state, load artwork, or document the shared atlas layout; they are identity/visual concerns rather than behavior differences.
