## Planned actions and source assets

### SitSettle — six frames per pet

- Marmalade output: `Assets/actions/Marmalade/sit-settle/0.png` through `5.png`

- Marmalade output: `Assets/actions/Marmalade/tail-flick/0.png` through `5.png`

### RollFlop — eight frames per pet

- Marmalade output: `Assets/actions/Marmalade/roll-flop/0.png` through `7.png`
- Merry output: `Assets/actions/Merry/roll-flop/0.png` through `7.png`
- Progression: neutral setup; begins leaning; forequarters lower; shoulder contacts ground; side flop; partial relaxed roll/exposure; settles slightly back; final relaxed side pose
- Motion will remain natural, readable, and grounded with intact paws/limbs and tail follow-through. The final frame may remain resting rather than returning upright.
- Merry will use the accepted Marmalade sequence as its frame-for-frame pose master.

All final frames will be 192 x 208 RGBA with real transparency, soft painterly/semi-realistic adult-cat anatomy, consistent scale, no checkerboard/chroma, no scene, and no labels/borders. Review sheets will exist only under `codex-checkpoints/action-previews/`.

## Generation and recovery approach

- Use built-in image generation with production atlases and accepted action sources as explicit identity/style/scale references.
- Generate and validate one action at a time, Marmalade first and Merry second.
- Prefer the proven flat saturated-magenta intermediate background so it can be removed through connected-background masking without color-keying orange, white, or gray fur.
- If an otherwise good result arrives with a separable flat/checker background, preserve the cat art and recover real alpha conservatively.
- Reject only material cat-art failures: broken anatomy, unusable motion, identity/style drift, impossible limbs/tail, or cleanup that would damage fur/whiskers/markings.
- Preserve every accepted earlier pack if a later pack fails.
