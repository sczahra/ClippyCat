# v2.0 Action Pack Art Plan

## Starting point

- Base commit: `0180f5110cfc0c7f0beb90988df53bff80946de4`
- The working tree contains the current uncommitted v1.9 pipeline milestone; this art run will preserve those files and add only source frames, review previews, and v2.0 art checkpoints.
- Production atlases: Marmalade and Merry, 1536x2496 RGBA, 192x208 cells, 8 columns.
- `Assets/actions/actions.json` defines Stretch and a disabled zero-frame Scratch placeholder only. It does not yet contain integration-ready definitions for this action pack, so `Import-Actions.ps1` will not be run and the manifest will not be guessed or edited.

## Production visual references

Rows and frames are zero-based.

- **Row 0, Idle, frames 0-5:** standing scale, front three-quarter camera, face/eye identity, closed-eye expressions in frames 2-3.
- **Rows 1-2, Run Right/Left, frames 0-7:** side-body anatomy, hind-leg articulation, tail curves, and lateral silhouette.
- **Row 3, Wave, frames 0-3:** stable upright body and lifted-paw articulation.
- **Row 4, Jump, frames 0-4:** crouched/low body, compressed hindquarters, airborne limb relationships, and recovery.
- **Row 5, Grooming, frames 0-7:** standing-to-lowered head/shoulder progression; frames 2-6 are the closest neck tuck and relaxed-eye references.
- **Row 6, Waiting, frames 0-5:** upright seated scale, grounding, front paws, chest shape, and tail placement.
- **Row 7, Pawing, frames 0-5:** seated lifted-forepaw articulation and subtle head/body shifts; useful only as an anatomical reference, not as a scratch substitute.
- **Row 8, Review, frames 0-5:** seated front-facing proportions, paw movement, and relaxed tail silhouette.
- **Rows 9-10, mouse-look directions, frames 0-7:** nearly static seated body with controlled head angles and several grounded tail curves.
- **Marmalade row 11, Stretch, frames 0-5:** approved lowering/extension/recovery rhythm and low-forequarters silhouette. Merry has no row-11 artwork.

## Action-by-action pose plan

### Scratch — 8 frames per pet (first priority)

- Primary references: row 6 frames 0/5 for seated setup and recovery; row 5 frames 2-6 for head/neck tuck; rows 1-2 for believable hind-leg bends; row 7 frames 1-4 for restrained body counter-motion.
- Sequence: neutral sit; hind leg lifts; paw reaches behind ear/neck; contact A; contact B; contact A; leg lowers; neutral recovery.
- Keep the planted forepaws and seated baseline stable. The scratching leg must remain anatomically attached with no duplicated paw.

### Yawn — 8 frames per pet

- Primary references: row 6 frames 0/5 for seated body; row 0 frames 2-3 and row 5 frames 4-5 for closed/relaxed eyes and facial fur.
- Sequence: neutral; mouth starts opening; wider; full yawn; held peak; closing; nearly closed; neutral recovery.
- Lock head size and markings. Only jaw/muzzle, eyelids, and a very small natural head lift should change.

### Sit-Settle — 6 frames per pet

- Primary references: row 6 frames 0-5 for upright sit; row 5 frames 2-6 for shoulder/head lowering; row 4 frames 0/4 and Marmalade row 11 frames 2-4 for compressed and lowered body relationships.
- Sequence: upright sit; shoulders soften; body lowers; forequarters settle; relaxed low posture; final settled/resting pose.
- Maintain one continuous baseline and avoid per-frame zoom.

### Tail-Flick — 6 frames per pet

- Primary references: row 6 frames 0/5 and row 8 frames 0-5 for the stable seated body; rows 9-10 frames 0-7 for existing tail curves and controlled head/body stillness.
- Sequence: neutral tail; small outward movement; farther sweep; opposite bend/peak; return sweep; neutral.
- Keep head, torso, and paws nearly pixel-stable in apparent placement; only the tail and minimal balance compensation should move.

### Roll-Flop — 8 frames per pet

- Primary references: row 4 frames 0-4 for crouch/compression; rows 1-2 for side anatomy; Marmalade row 11 frames 1-4 for lowering and recovery rhythm.
- Sequence: standing/seated setup; shoulder lowers; body tips; side contact; partial relaxed roll; roll back/settle; rise; recovery.
- Keep motion natural and low, without cartoon tumbling, rotation effects, or invented props/shadows.

## Identity preservation

### Marmalade

- Match the production orange-and-white tabby exactly: blue-green eyes, mature natural proportions, rounded face, white muzzle/chest/legs, orange saddle/striping, and thick ringed upright or grounded tail as pose requires.
- Preserve the soft painterly semi-realistic fur, camera angle, scale, and gentle neutral lighting.

### Merry

- Match the same pose progression and scale as the corresponding Marmalade action.
- Preserve the mostly white coat, soft gray crown patch, pink ears/nose, anatomical left eye green and anatomical right eye blue, slim natural proportions, and current facial identity.
- Do not copy Marmalade markings or introduce new gray body patches.

## Output and validation plan

- Generate one action sequence at a time, beginning with Scratch, using the production atlases as identity/style references.
- Use transparent intermediate sequence grids without text, labels, borders, checkerboards, backgrounds, or props; split selected frames into clean 192x208 RGBA source PNGs.
- Preserve full ears, paws, tail, whiskers, and fur edges; center and baseline consistently without destructive cropping.
- Validate exact dimensions/mode, alpha, frame count, nonempty bounds inside the canvas, absence of matte, scale consistency, and smooth silhouette progression.
- Save review-only contact sheets under `codex-checkpoints/action-previews/`, never inside source-frame folders.
- Stop rather than continue to later actions if Scratch identity, anatomy, or style cannot be held consistently.

## Artistic limitations and review risks

- There is no production hind-leg scratch pose; hind-leg/ear contact must be synthesized conservatively from seated and running anatomy.
- There is no production open-mouth face; Yawn requires careful muzzle/jaw generation and is at elevated identity-drift risk.
- There is no full side-lying roll pose; Roll-Flop has the highest anatomy and silhouette risk and should be attempted last.
- Merry lacks a dedicated Stretch row, so Marmalade row 11 can guide motion rhythm only; Merry identity must come exclusively from Merry production rows.
- Generative raster output may need targeted iteration for paws, eye laterality, alpha edges, and per-frame scale. Any unresolved defects will be called out rather than hidden or imported.

## Explicitly out of scope

- Editing `Program.cs`, `Assets/actions/actions.json`, importer scripts, project files, or production atlases
- Running `Import-Actions.ps1` or changing runtime action behavior
- New art direction, pixel art, vector/chibi styling, external stock sprites, backgrounds, labels, borders, text, or promotional sheets
- Generating Merry Stretch, changing existing Stretch art, or modifying any existing production row
