## Edit decision and operation

Row 6, frame 3 **was edited**. Before editing, its horizontal center and bottom were already nearly aligned; the mismatch was a combination of a modest scale difference and Merry's naturally slimmer silhouette. It was not primarily transparent padding or horizontal placement.

The complete antialiased Merry content was enlarged by approximately 4% within only that cell, using high-quality bicubic resampling. The transform was centered horizontally and bottom-anchored:

- horizontal scale: +3.8760%
- vertical scale: +4.1885% (integer-bound rounding)
- intended content bounds: from 129 x 191 at `(32,11)-(161,202)` to approximately 134 x 199 at `(29,4)-(163,203)`

No repainting, recoloring, pose change, or whole-atlas transform was used. The residual width difference was intentionally retained rather than distorting Merry's slimmer body and tail to exactly match Marmalade.

## Recommended manual visual test

1. Launch ClippyCat and select Merry.
2. Let Merry run until diagnostics report the `Waiting` state (row 6), or repeatedly use the existing action controls until that state appears.
3. Watch the transition through row 6 frames 2, 3, and 4 several times, ideally with a screen recording or slow playback. Confirm frame 3 no longer appears undersized and does not pop larger than its neighbors.
4. Confirm the paws/shadow stay on the same floor line and the body remains horizontally centered.
