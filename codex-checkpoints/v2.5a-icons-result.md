## Artwork and icon validation

- The app icon uses Marmalade and Merry together in a compact head-and-neck composition derived from the production row 7 front-facing artwork. The built-in ImageGen workflow was used with both production cats as identity/style references.
- The generated app composition initially contained a baked RGB checkerboard. A conservative connected subject mask removed only that backdrop; the final PNG is RGBA with real transparency and keeps fur and whisker detail intact.
- The tray icon is a separate, deliberately minimal front-facing cat-head silhouette with a white interior and near-black contour for light/dark taskbar contrast.
- Both ICO files contain 16, 20, 24, 32, 48, 64, 128, and 256 px images.
- Both 512x512 source PNGs and both ICO files have genuine alpha transparency.
- A review at 16-64 px confirmed the app artwork still reads as two cats and the tray silhouette remains distinct on light and dark backgrounds.

## Integration

- `ApplicationIcon` now embeds `ClippyCatAppIcon.ico` in the application executable, which also supplies the installed shortcut and Add/Remove Programs icon through the existing executable references.
- The Inno Setup script now sets `SetupIconFile` to the main ClippyCat app icon.
- `NotifyIcon` loads `Assets/Icon/ClippyCatTrayIcon.ico` relative to `AppContext.BaseDirectory`; missing or invalid files safely fall back to `SystemIcons.Application`.
- The loaded tray icon is disposed during normal application shutdown.
- The publisher requires both ICO files in the standalone payload. Published copies matched their source SHA-256 hashes exactly.
- No WinForms form icon override was needed because the pet window is borderless and does not appear in the taskbar.
