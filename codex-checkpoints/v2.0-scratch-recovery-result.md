## Cleanup method

- Modeled the baked background as two slightly varying neutral blue-gray checker families centered around RGB `(136, 139, 143)` and `(195, 197, 201)`.
- Classified only neutral pixels close to those families, then propagated the removal through checker-colored regions connected to each panel edge.
- Used the largest connected foreground silhouette per panel to reject isolated checker remnants.

- Added real alpha and used a narrow edge-aware transition. Only boundary pixels still close to the checker colors received nearest-interior matte cleanup; the cat interior was not globally keyed or recolored.
